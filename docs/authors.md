### About the author and maintainer

Daniel Semakula earned his medical degree from Makerere University in
Uganda and has spent most of his career as a physician-researcher. He
earned a Master of Public Health from the University of Manchester in
the United Kingdom and completed a PhD at the University of Oslo in
Norway. He worked at Johns Hopkins University, first as a postdoctoral
fellow and later as a Senior Research Data Analyst in the School of
Medicine’s Divisions of Hematology and General Internal Medicine. He has
more than 15 years of experience analysing data and advising on data
analysis for student- and staff-led research projects. He also serves as
an academic editor for two international journals.
